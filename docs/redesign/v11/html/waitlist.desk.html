The point is not that the right-hand dot is high. It is that the two dots are close. When we say 80%, we want it to happen about 80 times in 100 — more would mean we were being timid, less would mean we were overclaiming.

On purpose

Three things we do that cost us

  • We show the range, not just the number.Every forecast carries the spread around it, so you can see the ones we are sure of and the ones we are not.
  • We show nothing under 60%.A coin flip dressed as an insight is worse than an empty screen. When there is nothing worth showing, the screen says so.
  • We publish the misses.The record above counts every scored forecast, not a flattering selection of them.
